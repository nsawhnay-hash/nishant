━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

You've been grinding pull-ups, dips, and muscle-ups for months.
Your back is growing. Your chest is getting there.
But your shoulders? Just along for the ride.

Let me fix that.

In calisthenics, your side delts and rear delts are on permanent
vacation — and that's exactly how you build a body that looks
great from the front and completely falls apart from the side.

One bad rep away from benching yourself for months.

Here are the 3 moves that fix it:

✅ Lateral Raises (resistance band)
→ Wakes up your side delts. Builds the shoulder width you're
   missing from pull-ups and dips alone.

✅ Pike Push-Ups
→ Your first step to handstand strength. Overhead pressing
   power that transfers to every advanced calisthenics skill.

✅ Ring Face Pulls
→ Strengthens your rear delts and rotator cuff. The move
   99% of calisthenics athletes skip — until they get hurt.

Add these 2x per week. 3 sets each. Your shoulders stop being
the weakest link in every single movement you do.
